Private smart home integration

Home Assistant Nest

Home Assistant Nest is a private integration used by members of one household to connect their own Google Nest doorbell and supported Nest devices to their Home Assistant system.

The integration uses Google's Smart Device Management API to display supported device information and events and enable household automations. It is not a public service, does not offer public account registration, and is not affiliated with Google or the Home Assistant project.

Access is limited to people authorized by the household owner through Google consent and Home Assistant access controls.
