Home Assistant Nest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

About this integration

Home Assistant Nest is a private, non-commercial integration for one household. It connects household-owned Google Nest devices to a self-hosted Home Assistant installation. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or the Home Assistant project.

Information accessed

With the Google account holder's permission, the integration may access information that Google makes available through the Smart Device Management API, such as Nest device identifiers and status, camera or doorbell streams where authorized, and device events (for example, a doorbell press or detected motion).

How information is used

Information is used only to display the household's Nest devices in Home Assistant and to run explicitly configured household automations. The integration is not offered to the public. Google Cloud Pub/Sub may be used to deliver Nest device events to Home Assistant.

Storage and retention

Home Assistant may retain device states, events or related information in its local history, logs and backups depending on the household's configuration. Retention follows the applicable Home Assistant history, logging and backup settings. The integration does not independently operate a public database of users.

Sharing and security

Google provides device data through its Nest and Google Cloud services. Home Assistant processes it within the household's controlled installation. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with unrelated third parties. Access to Home Assistant is limited to authorized household members. Backup infrastructure, if configured, may store copies of Home Assistant data.

Your choices

The Google account holder can revoke the integration's Google account access through their Google account's connected apps settings. Home Assistant data can also be removed or retained according to the household's configuration and backup policies.
